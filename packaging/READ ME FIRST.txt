madhippu: financial statements out of Indian annual reports

HOW TO USE

1. Double-click "madhippu" (on Windows, "madhippu.exe").
2. A small window opens, then a page in your web browser.
3. Drop an annual report (PDF) onto that page.
4. See the statements, and download them as Excel.

Keep the small window open while you use madhippu; close it (or press
Quit on the page) when you're done. Your files never leave this computer.


THE FIRST TIME: YOUR COMPUTER WILL WARN YOU

madhippu isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so the
first time you open it your computer will say it can't check who made it.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Mac: "madhippu can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify…". Click
"Done", then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and
click "Open Anyway" next to madhippu. Confirm with your password.

You only need to do this once. If you'd rather check the program first,
its full source is at https://github.com/MuthuSubramanian00/madhippu
