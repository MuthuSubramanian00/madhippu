## Output can be wrong

madhippu extracts figures by software from documents it was not designed for
in advance. It checks its work (each section must add up to its printed
total), but checks can't catch every error, and source documents themselves
contain mistakes. In particular, madhippu may:

- misread, misplace or omit a figure, or assign it to the wrong period,
  basis (standalone or consolidated) or line item;
- read a document's layout or wording in a way the author never tested;
- produce output that looks complete when it isn't.

Always check any figure you rely on against the original document.
madhippu's output records the page each statement came from, and warnings
for anything it dropped or couldn't read, to make that easier.
